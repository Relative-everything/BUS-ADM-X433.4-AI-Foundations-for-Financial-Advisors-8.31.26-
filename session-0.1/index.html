The question that stays open

The Control Surface

What every setting actually does to one inference.

Tonight is a vocabulary lesson with a working model attached. By the end you will be able to look at an output and say which configuration produced it, which is a more durable skill than knowing where any of these switches currently live.

Context is assembled, additively, from named layers. Each control populates one of them.

The five layers

Every answer you have ever received from this product was produced from a context assembled out of these five layers, in this order, on that turn only. Nothing here is a property of the chat. It is a property of the turn.

Designed defects and known divergences, labelled here and on the page (pedagogy.md s1 R5)

  1. Section 6, three designed landmines. The persistence sorter contains three chips built to be mis-sorted: an incognito chat on a Team plan, a paused memory, and a deleted conversation. Each is labelled as a landmine on the page when it is solved. They are exercise material, not a trick.
  2. Section 4 and section 8, probe capture P2 names a client who is not in the case. The captured run was made against a prompt naming “David and Sarah Cole”. The canonical household in CASE.md is Meg and David Cole, and there is no Sarah Cole. The capture is shown verbatim because editing a transcript would make it a fabrication (R1). The divergence is labelled on screen and is itself teaching material: a fluent, professional, entirely wrong client name is exactly what this course exists to catch.
  3. Section 4 and section 8, probe capture P3 models figures that contradict CASE.md. The capture assumes a closely held interest of $12.0M with $1.2M of basis and a $1.2M seed gift. CASE.md states a pre-discount interest value of $7,200,000, basis of $240,000 on the founder stock, and a seed gift of $650,000. Shown verbatim, labelled on screen, and to be resolved by the instructor before delivery. Do not teach the capture’s numbers as the case’s numbers.
  4. Everything composed in the section 4 inspector is a construction. Only the three base outputs are captured runs. Every fragment the controls add is authored illustration, badged and chipped L wherever it appears.
