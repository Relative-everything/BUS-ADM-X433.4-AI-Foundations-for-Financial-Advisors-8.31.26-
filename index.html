This is the live course site. Every lesson is updated here directly — there are no files to download and no version numbers to track. Whatever you see on this page is current.

Each lesson is built as a core session of about an hour that always runs, followed by an appendix of optional depth. Gold links in the core point down to the appendix material that belongs at that moment; every appendix section links back. A control at the top of each lesson filters the appendix by depth — foundational, standard or advanced — or hides it entirely for the one-hour version.

Each lesson runs entirely in your browser. Your progress and any notes you enter stay on your own device and are never uploaded.

Course rule on client data

No real client data or personally identifying information enters any AI tool at any point in this course — including the build transcript submitted with the final project.

Every example across all five sessions uses one synthetic household, the Cole case, constructed for this course. No real client data appears anywhere on this site.
