# Part 1. What the migration had to do, and why

CASE.md v4.0 replaced the entire fact set. Three of the changes were
**inversions**: they void content rather than editing it, and a substitution
would have left the lessons asserting the opposite of the truth.

### The three inversions

**I1. Ohio to Illinois is not a name swap.** Ohio imposed no estate tax. Illinois
taxes estates above **$4,000,000**, does not index that threshold, does **not**
allow portability, permits an independent state QTIP election under
35 ILCS 405/2(b-1), and adds adjusted taxable gifts back in testing the
threshold, so the $2,002,000 seed gift counts against it. Every sentence reading
"Ohio domicile and therefore no state estate tax" was asserting the reverse of
the new position. Session 2's fourth cold-open card now carries that exposure,
and CASE.md's confidence labels travel with it: exclusion, portability and QTIP
at `[H]`, the rate table and the add-back at `[M]`.

**I2. QSBS and IRC §1202 are out of scope.** Deleted, not replaced, not
footnoted. The corpus held exactly one reference, a clause in a session 1 source
note using the 2025 amendment as a training-staleness example, plus a sample
commit message in `MAINTAINING.md`. The source note stands without it and no
section lost an interaction, so nothing was voided.

**I3. The grantor tax is the burn, not a burden.** Zero findings. The corpus
already framed it correctly in both places it appears (session 2's prompt
exercise and its citation triage both state that Meg's payment of the trust's tax
is not an additional gift). Nothing needed rewriting. Recorded because a null
result on an instructed check is a finding.

### The inversions that carried no retired string

These are the ones a grep of the Part K list would have missed entirely, and they
are the reason the inventory took a second pass.

- **"Founder and 100% owner", 8 occurrences.** Walter Hensley founded the company
  in 1987. Meg **bought** it on 1 July 2016 for $14,000,000, which is also her
  basis. She is not the founder and did not inherit the stock.
- **Three balance sheets omitted the $1,000,000 inherited IRA.** Incomplete, not
  merely stale, and it brings a second distribution clock with it.
- **The $18,000,000 / appraisal relationship is reversed.** In v4.0 $18,000,000
  is what the 2014 buy-sell formula *produces*, and the figure it conflicts with
  is the $55,000,000 indicated value. Every passage saying the buy-sell
  "conflicts with the $18,000,000 appraisal" had the stale document and the
  current one the wrong way round.
- **Re-identification items priced on "a metropolitan area the size of Dayton".**
  CASE.md carries no population figure for Rockford or anywhere else, so the size
  comparison was dropped rather than replaced with an invented number.
- **Session 3's retriever corpus spells its figures as words**: "thirty-one
  percent", "four million nine hundred sixty eight thousand dollars". No numeric
  search finds those.

# Part 3. Structural changes

### S1. The case block is generated, not maintained by hand
Sessions 0.1 and 1 each carried **two copies** of the case block, and session 1's
second copy cloned from the first by JavaScript at runtime. Sessions 2, 3, 4 and
the hub had **no case modal at all**; the hub carried orphan `#caseModal` CSS
with no markup.

New toolchain, all committed so the checks are reproducible rather than asserted:

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `CASE.md` | master, the only file edited by hand |
| `scripts/build-case.mjs` | CASE.md → the generated artifacts. Every figure is captured from a **named section** by an anchored pattern; a pattern that stops matching is a hard failure, which **is** the drift detection. Recomputes 13 Part M identities. |
| `scripts/case-facts.json` | generated, 102 keyed figures |
| `scripts/case-extract.html` | generated, the one-screen extract |
| `scripts/case-flowchart.html` | generated, Part L scoped under `.cole-flow` |
| `scripts/inject-case.mjs` | replaces the span between the CASE sentinels in each of the six files |
| `scripts/verify-case.mjs` | SHA-256 per injected block, non-zero exit on drift |

Sentinels inserted in all six files; none had them. One region per lesson.
Idempotence proved: two runs produce a **zero-byte diff**. Drift detection proved
by tampering: altering one character inside a block fails `verify-case.mjs` and
names the file.

Exercise code reads figures from an inlined `COLE` constant generated from
`case-facts.json`, so a number typed into exercise logic is now the defect the
build prevents rather than the norm.

### S2. The Structure tab
Part L ships as a second tab in the case modal in every file, keyboard-reachable
with a visible focus state, both sheets, all four `<title>`/`<desc>` preserved,
no scripts, no CDN, no external assets. Verified standalone in headless Chromium:
zero JS errors, zero network requests.

### S3. Titles
410 headings and labels swept. 7 content titles rewritten toward literal
descriptive noun phrases; 29 task labels converted on a second pass at the
instructor's direction. 405 of 410 now compliant. The five still matching the
detector are false positives where "Run" and "Rebuild" are nouns
("Run 1 fallback") and one compound adjective.

Zero titles matched the hype patterns. The corpus was already close to compliant
on this axis and session 4 already carried the worked example
"Nonpublic Personal Information Under Regulation S-P".

# Part 6. The spine

Part K deleted the four-session framing question, whether the planning window is
still open and whether a 2023 appraisal still supports a 31% discount, and
forbade reintroducing it, supplying no replacement.

`docs/spine-brief.md` (434 lines) inventories the **nine sockets** the old spine
occupied, assembles the raw material from CASE.md with section references,
computes the annual decision, and ranks three candidates.

**The hypothesis the brief was asked to test, not assume, is CONFIRMED.** Meg
goes structurally cash-negative in steady state while the structure performs
exactly as designed. The crossover is exact and it is **year 6**: year 5 is the
last positive year at +$2,266,953 and year 6 is the first negative one at
−$522,086 on CASE.md's own accounting, −$543,247 to −$550,111 on a full
accounting of Part D. Nothing has gone wrong when that happens. Every memorandum
assumption holds. The shortfall **is** the mechanism: Meg has transferred 90% of
the units and kept the obligation to pay tax on 100% of the income.

Also computed: calling the gap each year exhausts the $20,020,000 note in
**structure year 30, at age 94**, because each $1,000,000 called permanently
removes $38,200 of future interest and the gap therefore widens by 3.82% of every
call.

**Implemented at the instructor's direction**, candidate A with candidate C's
lever clause:

> Meg is short **$522,086** a year from year 6, while the structure performs
> exactly as designed. **How much of the $20,020,000 note does she call this
> year, what does calling it cost her in every year after, and when is a
> different lever the better answer?**

It lives in `inject-case.mjs`, not in CASE.md, deliberately: CASE.md supplies the
arithmetic and draws no conclusion, and a course-level question is not a case
fact. One edit reaches all six files. Every `VOID: needs replacement` marker is
now closed. Per-session angles: 0.1 asks which configuration you would trust with
a compounding schedule at all; 1 shows why a language model is worst at exactly
that shape of problem; 2 asks which document governs the number the structure
rests on; 3 requires the decision **and its forward cost** to be documented;
4 treats the annual call as an unfiled position whose audit trail is the artifact.

**No lesson answers it, and none may.** CASE.md draws no conclusion about whether
the structure is advisable.

# Part 7. Defects this migration introduced and then caught

Recorded because a migration that claims to have introduced none has not looked.

### N1. The Structure panel would have broken under Shift+U
The injected panel used the corpus-wide `.hidden` class. The Shift+U instructor
override strips **every** `.hidden` on the page by design, so pressing it would
have revealed both case tabs stacked on top of each other. Caught by the browser
battery. The panels now use their own `.case-off` class, which the override
cannot reach, and the battery asserts it:
*"Shift+U does not reveal both case tabs at once"*.

### N2. Regenerating the changelog blanked its style fence
`build-changelog.py` ships an empty fence and documents `restyle_sweep` as the
required follow-up. The fence was restored to origin/main's exact bytes at the
time, and later brought current with the rest by the sanctioned sweep.

### N3. Spine figures were literals in hand-edited prose
The generated block reads every figure from `case-facts.json` and cannot drift.
The recurring question also appears in hand-written prose in sessions 1, 2 and 4,
where the figures are literals: $522,086, $20,020,000, $38,200. Nothing was
checking those; if CASE.md changed, the generated block would follow and those
three paragraphs silently would not. **Check 20** now pins each to its fact.
Proved by tampering: changing one to $530,000 fails the battery naming the file,
the label and both values.

### N4. A stated result was wrong in a commit message
The Phase 5 commit claimed "static battery 13 of 13 pass". It was 12 of 13.
Corrected in a follow-up commit rather than by amending history.

# Part 10. Deferred, and why

### DF1. The 60-minute core with a 13-interaction floor
The instructor's rule is a core lesson of 60 minutes carrying at least 13
interactions, with everything else reachable through the appendix selector.
Current state:

| Session | Core min | Core interactions | Appendix | Selector |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | 120 | 14 | none | **absent** |
| 1 | 67 | **10** | 166 min / 7 | present |
| 2 | 67 | **11** | 141 min / 5 | present |
| 3 | 70 | 13 | 128 min / 4 | present |
| 4 | 70 | 13 | 123 min / 5 | present |

60 minutes with 13 interactions is one interaction every 4.6 minutes, denser than
anything in the corpus today. Sessions 3 and 4 nearly satisfy it. Sessions 1 and
2 are **below** the floor. Every route to compliance is a curriculum decision:
moving a section to the appendix takes its interactions out of core and makes the
count worse, and adding interactions to reach 13 is padding. Deferred at the
instructor's direction.

### DF2. Session 0.1's appendix
120 minutes, 12 sections, 14 interactions, no appendix and no selector. Splitting
it requires deciding which sections are core. Deferred: 0.1 is a work in progress
and is not needed for this class.

### DF3. Probe capture P3
Two sections carry `[SUPERSEDED BY ILLINOIS]`. Assessed by the instructor as low
risk: it is an example of model *output*, not a source of case facts, and it is
labelled. Re-run alongside the rest of session 0.1.

### DF4. Confidence chips without a `data-src` key
3, 11, 8, 4 and 19 across sessions 0.1 to 4. **Identical to origin/main**: none
introduced here. This is finding M3 from the 2026-08-20 audit, still partly open.
Zero dangling keys, so every key that exists resolves.
