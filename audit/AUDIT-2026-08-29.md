the entry it names. **An errata-entry mechanism is under consideration for

**Verified programmatically:** every commit referenced below was resolved with

## How severity was decided

**Material.** A substantive change to a served page, a governance document, a
validator or generator, or a register is **entirely** absent from the entry; or
two or more distinct merged branches are presented as one undifferentiated
entry; or the entry states something the diff contradicts.

**Cosmetic.** The substance is present, but granularity sits below the
convention the new entries follow: no SHA cited, no branch named, no per-commit
breakdown, files not listed.

The yardstick is the convention the new entries adopt: one entry per merged
phase, with per-commit sub-detail (SHA, one-line substance, files touched where
informative), cross-referencing the register rows each commit opened or closed,
and naming the branch each phase used.

## CONTESTED severities: ruling deferred to Jared at triage

Two findings turn on the same unresolved question, and both are recorded
**Cosmetic** on a call that is open to reversal. **Neither has been ruled on. The
ruling is Jared's at triage, and both move to Material if he takes the other
side.**

| Finding | Entry | Recorded as |
|---|---|---|
| `746eba9`, session-2 A9 entity baseline lowered by hand, 67 to 66 | 2026-08-25 · Case unification | Cosmetic |
| `d21f0eb`, four A9 ratchet figures lowered after the Phase 2 furniture retirement | 2026-08-25 (bare) | Cosmetic |

**The case for Cosmetic, which is how they are recorded.**
`scripts/editorial-baseline.json` is neither a validator script nor a member of
the repository's own REGISTER set (`CASE.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`,
`changelog/index.html`, `docs/editorial-gap-report.md`,
`docs/repo-updates-plan.md`), so neither clause of the Material test reaches it
by its letter. In both cases the underlying content change that moved the figure
*is* described in the entry; what is missing is the bookkeeping consequence.

**The case for Material.** The file is the recorded state four ratchets run
against, and this repository's own governance, added later at `93470bb`, holds
that a lowering is exactly the event that goes unnoticed: *"a raised figure is
impossible by rule; a lowered one is legitimate and therefore invisible, because
the check goes on passing."* That rule requires a register row in the same
commit. A changelog that records neither lowering leaves the corpus measured
against a slacker bar with no reader-facing trace. Against that reading, a
silent ratchet lowering is precisely a substantive change to a validator's
recorded state, and Material is the right call.

**Note on anachronism, which cuts for Cosmetic.** The `93470bb` rule postdates
both commits. Judging a 2026-08-25 entry by a 2026-08-26 rule is not obviously
fair, and `DW-045` was opened later to record three pre-rule lowerings
retroactively. `746eba9`'s lowering is not among the three that `DW-045`
carries.
