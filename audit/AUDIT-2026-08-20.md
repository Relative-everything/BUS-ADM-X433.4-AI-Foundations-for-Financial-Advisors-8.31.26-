# P2 — Major. Violates a non-negotiable or a ratified parameter.

### M3. The chip-to-footer evidence contract has never been implemented
**Severity** Major (pedagogy R2, non-negotiable) · **Confidence** H · **Pre-existing**

138 `.conf` chips across the four files. **Zero** carry `data-src`. **Zero**
footer `<li id="src-KEY">` entries exist. Per evidence-standards.md, chips
without keys "are decoration" — a reader cannot get from a claim to the source
that grounds it, which is precisely the discipline the course teaches.

The in-section `.src` lines (layer 1) are good and mostly complete. What is
missing is layer 2 and the wiring between them.

Footer format also diverges four ways: S1 prose paragraph (0 `<li>`), S2 20
numbered entries, S3 11 numbered entries, S4 prose paragraph (0 `<li>`).

**Fix, in order:** (a) give S1 and S4 numbered footers matching S2's format;
(b) assign `id="src-KEY"` to every footer entry; (c) add `data-src` to every
chip. Step (c) is mechanical once (a) and (b) exist. This is the single
largest quality gap in the corpus and the one most visible to a sceptical
reader, because the course's own thesis is that fluent and correct look
identical.

### M4. The legal/tax `.verify` gate exists only in Session 1
**Severity** Major (pedagogy R3, non-negotiable) · **Confidence** H · **Pre-existing**

R3 requires every legal, tax, and regulatory characterisation to sit behind a
`.verify` block so the instructor signs it before teaching it as settled. S1
has one. S2, S3 and S4 have none — and they carry the heaviest legal content
in the course:

- **S3**: federal Wiretap Act one-party floor, the ten/eleven/twelve/fifteen
  all-party state disagreement, Cal. Penal Code § 637.2 damages, the
  stricter-state rule for cross-border recording.
- **S4**: Regulation S-P 2024 amendments and both compliance dates, the 30-day
  notification clock, FINRA 2026 Oversight Report, SEC FY2026 Priorities §VII,
  IRC §§ 1014 / 2010 / 691 / 7520.
- **S2**: IRC § 671 grantor-trust authority, Rev. Proc. 2017-34, 2022-32.

**Fix:** add a `.verify` block to each of S2, S3, S4 listing its specific
characterisations. S1's block is the template.

### M5. All four sessions miss the 150-minute parameter
**Severity** Major (s4, tolerance 0) · **Confidence** H · **I introduced/worsened this**

146 / 125 / 112 / 110 against a ratified 150 exactly. S3 and S4 summed to 150
before my restructure; S2 was 142. I allocated core and appendix minutes
independently and never re-summed to the parameter.

The practical consequence is not the validator — it is that the "full block"
version of each session now varies by 36 minutes, so a 150-minute room gets a
very different experience in S1 than in S4.

**Fix:** rebalance so core + appendix = 150 in every session. S1 needs −4 from
its 83-minute appendix; S2 +25; S3 +38; S4 +40. The shortfalls in S3 and S4
are large enough that they should be filled with *new* appendix material
rather than by inflating existing chips — see E17.

### M6. The cold-open ritual exists only in Session 1
**Severity** Major (s3.8 ritual stability) · **Confidence** H · **Pre-existing**

Family 17 requires the cold open be "identical every session"; that is the

S1's own instance is also under-budget at 4 min versus the ratified 8.

### M7. Work-along gates exist only in Session 1
**Severity** Major (family 1, one per section minimum) · **Confidence** H · **Pre-existing**

S1 carries 20 `data-gate` cues. S2, S3, S4 carry zero. Three of four sessions

### M8. The new Session 4 appendix section has no interaction

### Q13. The style fence is missing from all four files
**Confidence** H · **Pre-existing.** No `STYLE:BEGIN/END` markers, so
`restyle_sweep.py` cannot manage any lesson. A repo-wide restyle — the whole

two different minute-chip classes (`.mins` in S1/S3/S4, `.clock` in S2) and
four different footer formats.

### Q14. No `data-task` / `data-comp` tagging anywhere
**Confidence** H · **Pre-existing.** Component diversity, the 13–15 interaction
budget, and the no-adjacent-repeats rule cannot be validated. My inventory had
to infer families from CSS-class signatures. Tagging is ~90 attribute
additions across the corpus and makes every future audit scriptable.

### Q15. Prose density runs 1.4–1.8× the proposed band
**Confidence** M (method unratified, Part C — reported, never blocking).
S1 67.4, S2 73.6, S3 57.9, S4 68.8 words per allocated minute against a
proposed 37–42. Read against my earlier trim work, this says the same thing
from the other direction: these sessions carry more prose than their minutes
support, and the honest fix is more minutes or fewer words, not tighter
sentences. Worth ratifying the band before acting on it.

### Q16. Appendix return path is broken for in-place teaching
**Confidence** H · **I introduced this — design flaw, not a bug**

Every appendix section links back to the core section it came *from*. If you
teach §02, jump to Appendix A1, and finish it, the only link takes you back to
§02 — the section you already taught. To reach §03 you scroll manually past
the whole appendix. Scrolling down from A1 lands in A2, which belongs
somewhere else entirely.

The bidirectional contract I built is right for reading and wrong for
teaching. **Fix:** make the appendix header carry both ends — "← back to §02 ·
continue to §03 →". One extra anchor per appendix section.

# Enhancements — appendix architecture

### E24. Implement the complexity tiers (you asked for this; it is scaffolded and empty)
`data-tier` exists on all 20 appendix sections and every value is
`"standard"`. The intended model — a basic core, with advanced appendix slides
reachable when the room can take them — is not yet expressible. Concretely:
tag each appendix section `foundational` / `standard` / `advanced`, add a
three-state filter in the topbar that dims out-of-tier appendix cards, and
have the pacing panel recompute minutes for the selected tier. That turns
"core 63 / appendix 83" into "core 63 / +foundational 22 / +standard 34 /
+advanced 27", which is the number an instructor actually needs at 7pm.

### E25. Fill the S3 and S4 appendices rather than inflating them
Hitting 150 (M5) needs +38 in S3 and +40 in S4. Do not pad existing chips.
Candidates already implied by the corpus: S3 — a hybrid-search appendix
(lexical + vector, the fix Anthropic's own numbers point to) and a
re-ranking-pass demo; S4 — restore the Part 1 completeness gate as a core
section (C2) and add a Reg S-P incident-clock tabletop.

### E26. Give the appendix a core-only print and export path
There is no way to hand a student the core session alone. `@media print`
hides the rail and topbar but prints every appendix section. A
`body.core-only` class toggled from the topbar, plus a print rule that drops
`section.apx`, gives you the one-hour handout for free.

### E27. Rebalance S1's appendix
83 minutes across 7 sections is the largest appendix in the course and holds
the two best labs (A2 hand-fit, A5 sampler). S1's core is also the thinnest at
63 minutes. Consider promoting A2 or A5 into core and demoting Tier choice
(§07), which duplicates decision work S4 does more rigorously.
