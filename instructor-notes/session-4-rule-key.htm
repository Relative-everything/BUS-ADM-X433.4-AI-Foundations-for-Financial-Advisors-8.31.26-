SESSION 4 · THE RULE KEY · §02'S NINE, IN SORTER ORDER

binds   a twin rule does the same job   no  ·  H / M / L = how sure
No US securities rule was written for AI. Your registration decides which duties are yours; the CFP marks add CFP Board's Code on top.
RulePassed · in forceWhat it doesRIABDCFPVendorAI?Say it · the trap
Lane 2 · A duty you already have · items 1 to 5 · binds you now, written before AI, reaches AI work unchanged
1Regulation S-P
SEC · 17 CFR Part 248		 2000 · amended May 2024
In force: 3 Dec 2025 ($1.5B+ RIAs), 3 Jun 2026 smaller: in force for all now M		Protect client NPI. Since 2024: a written incident response program, clients told as soon as practicable, within 30 days of awareness, vendor contracts that report to the firm within 72 h. No 24 h CCO clock.General"Pasting client data into a tool is a disclosure." It needs a contract limiting the vendor's use; a consumer plan has none. "She is my client" is NPI by itself.
2Fiduciary duty
Advisers Act §206 · 15 USC 80b-6		 1940 · duty from Capital Gains, 1963 · SEC restated 2019 HDuty of care (best interest, a reasonable basis, monitoring) and loyalty, for all advice.General"The duty attaches to the advice, not the tool." Unchecked AI output is advice with no reasonable basis. Twins: Reg BI (BD), A.1 (CFP).
3FINRA Rule 3110
Supervision		 In force 1 Dec 2014 HA supervisory system and written procedures reasonably designed to catch violations.General"Your staff's AI work is still their work." A binding rule, not guidance: the rule 24-09 points at. Advisers' twin: 206(4)-7, §203(e)(6).
4FINRA Rule 2210
Communications with the public		 In force 4 Feb 2013 · AI FAQs May 2024 MFair, balanced, not misleading. Retail (25+ retail investors in 30 days): principal pre-approves. Correspondence: reviewed, kept.General"An AI-drafted email is a firm communication." One client email is correspondence: no pre-approval. Advisers' twin: the Marketing Rule.
5Books and records
Advisers 204-2 · BDs 17a-4, FINRA 4511		 204-2 since 1961 MKeep what supports advice and business mail: 5 yrs advisers, 3 yrs BD mail, 6 yrs FINRA default.General"A prompt and output that support advice are records." No rule names prompts yet. Say "treat as", not "the rule says".
Lane 3 · Guidance or a proposal · items 6 to 8 · names AI, binds nobody
6FINRA Notice 24-09
Regulatory Notice, generative AI		 27 Jun 2024 · still active, not rescinded MReminds member firms the existing rules apply to generative AI. No new requirement.Names AI"No new requirement. The existing rules apply." Naming AI does not make it binding. It is a signpost to rows 3 to 5.
7SEC exam priorities
FY2026, Division of Examinations, §VII		 17 Nov 2025 · states it has "no legal force or effect" MWhat examiners test: AI claims, AI supervision, Reg S-P readiness, polymorphic malware.Names AI"No duty. It tells you which duties get checked." Not a rule; the rules under it bind. FY2027 not found as of 11 Oct 2026: check before class.
8SEC analytics proposal
Predictive data analytics, S7-12-23		 Proposed Jul 2023 · withdrawn 12 Jun 2025, with 13 others MWould have made firms neutralise conflicts in AI tools used with investors.Names AI"The one US proposal that would have named the technology. Withdrawn: binds nobody."
Lane 1 · A rule written for AI · item 9 · names AI, binds someone, not you
9EU AI Act, Art. 50
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689		 In force Aug 2024 · Art. 50 2 Aug 2026; older tools 2 Dec 2026 MAI providers mark AI output machine-readably; deployers label deepfakes.AI law"Binding law that names AI. Not a US securities rule." It is why Claude marks its text. It binds Anthropic, not a US adviser with US clients.

If you are asked

  • "Does the vendor owe the 72 h?" No, the firm does: the rule makes its policies require it M.
  • "Are prompts records?" Unresolved. FINRA's 2026 report suggests prompt and output logs M. Keep them.
  • "Is pre-approval going away?" Proposed Jul 2026, Notice 26-14 M. Not adopted.
  • "Who can read Claude's mark?" Bodies EU law names, and firms the Act obliges. Anthropic says it carries no identifying information H.
