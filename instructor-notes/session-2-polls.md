# Session 2 Zoom polls

One poll per core section, each launched before the on-page step it mirrors. Build and test all four ahead; students are not co-hosts. Questions under 255 characters, answers under 100. If a poll fails, take the answer in chat on a count of three, read the tally aloud and apply the same rule. The Case 2 commit (`sCase`) and the two `s12d` votes are chat waterfalls, not polls.

## Poll 1
- **Section:** `s6b`, opens it. **Format:** ranking. **Zoom:** Rank Order, four items.
- **Question:** Rank the four parts of a prompt by how much each changes whether the answer is correct, most to least.
- **Options:** Persona (who it writes as) / Task (the one operation) / Context (the facts and the source boundary) / Format (shape, length, structure).
- **Expected:** Persona last: 162 roles, four model families, 2,410 factual questions, no accuracy gain (Zheng and colleagues 2024; SOURCES `src-zheng-persona`, H). Context first, Task and Format between, is the course's order in the Appendix B3 table: a position, not a measurement.
- **Rule:** If more than half rank Persona first or second, read the Zheng result in one breath and run the room call with Persona left at Absent. Otherwise reveal and move on.

## Poll 2
- **Section:** `s9`, opens it, before the sorter. **Format:** multi-select. **Zoom:** Multiple choice, select all, seven answers.
- **Question:** Which steps of the seven-step planning process stay with the planner? A model may draft words for them but cannot perform them. Select all that apply.
- **Options:** 1 Understand the client's circumstances / 2 Identify and select goals / 3 Analyze the current course and alternatives / 4 Develop the recommendations / 5 Present the recommendations / 6 Implement the recommendations / 7 Monitor and update.
- **Expected:** Steps 2 and 4, the page's key (3 and 7 drafted then verified; 1, 5 and 6 assisted at the margin). It is the lesson's position and the sorter invites argument. The adoption figure under the sorter is FACTS:adoption-t3-2026 (M).
- **Rule:** If fewer than half select step 4, read the page's reason for it (credential, registration and liability attach to it) before the sorter opens. If more than half also pick step 1 or 5, say the key sorts both as assist and hold that argument for the sorter. Otherwise reveal and move on.

## Poll 3
- **Section:** `s10`, opens it; launch before reading the sentence that says three of the six fail the usual check. **Format:** numeric-range prediction. **Zoom:** Single choice, four ranges.
- **Question:** Six citations came back when an AI assistant was asked what authority sets the age at which Meg and David must start required distributions. How many would you have accepted as authority as written?
- **Options:** 0 or 1 / 2 / 3 or 4 / 5 or 6.
- **Expected:** 2. Items 1 and 2 (SECURE 2.0 section 107; T.D. 10001) are sound; item 3 is the right section, wrong cohort; items 4 and 5 are IRS instructions cited as law; item 6 does not exist. Source: the `s10` key; CASE.md Part F.1, F.4, F.6 and E.1.
- **Rule:** If more than half pick 3 or more, run the triage and call on a "5 or 6" voter for the item they trusted first. If more than half pick 0 or 1, reveal 2 and say checking costs time, which the live run measures. Otherwise reveal and move on.

## Poll 4
- **Section:** `s12d`, opens Phase one, ahead of the sealed vote. **Format:** single choice, the advisor-fee poll. **Zoom:** Single choice, four answers.
- **Question:** A household with $2,446,000 to invest takes 30 hours of planner time a year. Which pay arrangement pays the planner the most per hour worked?
- **Options:** A. $300 an hour / B. 1% of assets, $24,460 a year / C. A flat $12,000 annual retainer / D. About the same: it averages out.
- **Expected:** B, about $815 an hour against $300 (A) and $400 (C). With 6 hours saved, B rises to about $1,019, C to $500, and A stays at $300 an hour on $1,800 less revenue. The 1%, $300 and $12,000 are hypotheticals; $2,446,000 is the Coles' investable total (CASE.md Part C.1). No FACTS record covers fee models; the answer rests on the stated inputs.
- **Rule:** If more than half pick B, thirty seconds on the arithmetic, then the vote. If more than a third pick A or D, divide $24,460 by 30 on the board and ask who keeps the six hours under each arrangement. If one option passes three quarters, cap the fee exchange at three minutes.
- **Provenance:** Reused from Fall 2026 (Session 2, the compensation poll, one of the term's two best openers) in subject and form. The Fall wording was not saved and it had no reveal; the room agreed within a minute. This version adds the reveal and the cap.
