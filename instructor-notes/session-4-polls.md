# Session 4 Zoom polls: seven to build

Rewritten 2026-09-28, late afternoon, for the clock on the teaching aid of the same date (three
pages; the packages went out last week, three off-repo activities and a breakout block after §07).
Build all seven in the Zoom web portal before class; a poll configured live was retracted in
Session 2. Each block is laid out the way Zoom's poll editor asks for it: title, question, one
option per line, single choice. Name each poll by its slot time so the launch order reads off the
polls panel. Set **Anonymous** only where marked. Every field is under Zoom's 255-character limit.

A tick marks the answer the section lands; "habits check" means there is no right answer and the
result is the lesson. Two things that look like polls are not: the Real or Not scores at 7:39 go in
chat as a number, and the D4 vote at 8:29 is the page's own widget, launched twice on the page.

## Poll 1 · 6:12 · Cold open

```
Title: 6:12 · Which would you have sent?
Question: Four prompts about the Cole case are on the page. Which one would you have sent from your own AI account this morning?
A · Prompt A
B · Prompt B
C · Prompt C
D · Prompt D
```
Lands: D is the one you could not send. It passes 5 of the 8 checks and carries five identifiers:
her name, 64, the company, $55 million, the letter. Most of the room picks D because it is the
most useful prompt. Say on the result: "Good and allowed are two tests. D passed the first and
failed the second; click it and watch it sink."

## Poll 2 · 6:46 · §03, two questions in one poll

```
Title: 6:46 · Your tool, and whose name is on it
Question 1: Which AI tool do you use most for work?
A · Claude
B · ChatGPT
C · Copilot
D · Gemini
E · None yet
Question 2: Whose name is on the agreement for that tool?
A · Mine: I clicked Accept on a personal plan
B · My firm's: an officer signed a Team or Enterprise agreement
C · A developer's: it runs through an API key
D · I do not know
```
Habits check. Click the majority vendor on the map and the majority plan tile. Say on the result:
"If you answered A to the second question, your firm signed nothing, and the switch that keeps your
prompts out of training is one you had to find yourself. The ladder shows what the firm's signature
adds, rung by rung."

## Poll 3 · 7:16 · The breach index · ANONYMOUS

```
Title: 7:16 · Your work email in the breach index
Question: You just checked your own work email at haveibeenpwned.com, privately. How many breaches did it list?
A · 0
B · 1 to 3
C · 4 to 10
D · 11 or more
E · I did not check
```
Anonymous: ON. Habits check; the distribution is the lesson. Say on the result: "A password from
any of those, reused on the AI account, and the assistant with the Drive connector is now theirs.
Not a deepfake. A login. Rung 5 on the ladder is the firm doing two-step for everyone; on plan A
you do it yourself, tonight."

## Poll 4 · 7:33 · §05, before the assistant runs

```
Title: 7:33 · The assistant
Question: Your assistant reads your email and files, and can send email. You ask: "Summarise today's emails." What comes back?
A · Just the summary
B · The summary, and something else happens
```
Lands: B. Say nothing about the hidden line until the x-ray; the poll must not give the attack
away. Say on the result, after the run: "It read a line you never saw as an instruction, because
it is text like everything else it reads. Wording is not a lock; access is. Fixes 2 and 3 are
settings."

## Poll 5 · 7:43 · The progression, before the clip plays

```
Title: 7:43 · The first year you would have believed
Question: You are about to see the same AI-made scene remade year by year. Which year's clip is the first you would have believed was a real video?
A · 2023
B · 2024
C · 2025
D · 2026
E · None of them
```
Launch before anything plays; hide the results until the clip has run. Adjust the years to the
ones the clip labels. Say on the result: "Most of you named a year that is already behind us.
Seeing is not proof. The callback to the number on file is the control, and it takes ninety
seconds."

## Poll 6 · 7:55 · D5, the clock

```
Title: 7:55 · The latest lawful day
Question: Your firm becomes aware that a client file was reached by someone outside. Under Regulation S-P, what is the latest lawful day to tell the client?
A · 3 days
B · 30 days from the day you knew
C · 45 days
D · When the investigation is complete
E · Only once harm is confirmed
```
Lands: B. Say on the result: "Thirty days from awareness, and an inconclusive investigation does
not excuse the notice. The vendor owes your firm 72 hours; your firm owes the client 30 days;
Equifax took 40."
