# Session 5 Zoom polls: four to build, one launched twice

Each poll is written to paste straight into Zoom (question under 255 characters, answers under
100). The slot time is the run sheet's. A tick marks the answer the section lands; "habits check"
means there is no right answer and the result is the lesson. Every figure is on the page or in the
verified facts behind it. Updated 2026-10-04 for the page with §07 (the firm's library): the second
half moves five minutes later, so Poll 4 is at 8:47.

**Run all four.** They sit at 6:09, 6:28, 8:15 and 8:47: one before each half of the page and one
to open and close the discussion.

---

### Poll 1 · 6:09 · Cold open
**Question:** Two prompts about the same client are on the page. In your first week of this course, which one would you have sent?
**Answers:** A · Then / B · Now
**Lands:** habits check. Most rooms pick Then.
**Say on the result:** "Then scores 3 of 8 and names the client. Now scores 8 of 8 and names no one. The distance between them is what five weeks changed in how you ask."

### Poll 2 · 6:28 · §03, before the run
**Question:** Six inputs are about to run through the meeting-prep brief. Which do you think break it? Pick up to three.
**Answers:** 1 · The sample note / 2 · Appraisal sentence removed / 3 · Two years of notes pasted in / 4 · A pasted email that contradicts the CRM / 5 · A real note with name and account number / 6 · The facts as a table
**Lands:** 2, 4, 5 ✓.
**Say on the result:** "Three broke, in three different ways: a gap filled, a conflict resolved in silence, and client data carried straight through. The input that breaks it is the finding."

### Poll 3 · 8:15 · §05, before the reveal
**Question:** Across four published trials that timed people with and without AI, how much faster were they on average?
**Answers:** A · 40% or more / B · 20 to 40% / C · 0 to 20% / D · Slower
**Lands:** habits check; the spread is the lesson (40% faster to 19% slower).
**Say on the result:** "Writers 40% faster, consultants 25%, support agents 14% more per hour, and experienced developers 19% slower while believing they were 20% faster. Felt faster is not faster. Time it."

### Poll 4 · 8:47 · E5, the sealed vote and the re-vote (launch twice)
**Question:** As the tool does more of the analysis, an adviser's value moves to the relationship. Agree or disagree?
**Answers:** A · Agree / B · Lean agree / C · Lean disagree / D · Disagree
**Lands:** no right answer; the second vote against the first is the result.
**Say on the result:** "Same room, same sentence, one fact added: the top reason clients fire an adviser is the quality of the advice, and perceived quality includes how it was explained. Watch which way the room moved."

---

**Building them in Zoom:** Polls 1, 3 and 4 are single-choice, one question each. Poll 2 is
multiple-choice with up to three picks. Poll 4 is one poll launched twice. Name each poll by its
number and slot time so the launch order is obvious in the polls panel.

**If you want a fifth:** §08's Rank the Reasons works as a chat harvest instead of a poll: "Top
reason clients fire an adviser, one word, in chat" before anyone locks the list. The page then
shows the survey's order (advice 32%, relationship 21%, cost 17%, returns 11%, going it alone 10%,
communication 9%).
