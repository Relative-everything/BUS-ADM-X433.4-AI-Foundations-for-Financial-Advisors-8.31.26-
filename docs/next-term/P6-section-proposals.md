## Red team

Strongest case against: a 60-minute core with a 10-minute opener, a 7-minute bridge and a 15-minute discussion leaves 28 minutes of taught sections, so the sessions lean on the case pages being done before class. If students arrive without having opened the case page, `sCase` degrades to the narrated case the evidence warns against. Mitigation in Phase 7: the case commit is re-askable in two minutes on the lesson page.
Assumption most likely wrong: that Fall 2026's dead zones were caused by the sections rather than by the overflow that pushed every session late. If overflow was the cause, the demotions still help (less core), but some demoted sections (Session 4 `s5` Attacks, Session 3 `s9` Workflow) may deserve to come back once the core is reliably taught.
