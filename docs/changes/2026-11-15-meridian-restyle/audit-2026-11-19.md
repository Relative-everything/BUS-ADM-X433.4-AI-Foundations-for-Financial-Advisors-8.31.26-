## 2. UI: one defect class found and fixed

`layout-audit.mjs` counts four kinds of layout defect in both versions and fails where the branch has more.

**Found.** Step 2 had snapped every font size onto the type scale, including sizes on text inside SVG drawings. Those sizes are in the drawing's own units and the labels were placed for them. Snapped up (9.5 to 12.5, 11 to 12.5, 13 to 14), labels overlapped and ran outside their figures: 18 labels outside a drawing (0 before) and 27 more overlapping pairs, on Session 5 (`#e2Fig`, `#s2Fig`, `#s2Meter`, `#s3Fig`, `#s4Fig`) and two labels on Session 2. This is the failure the handback's red team named and the screenshots I had sampled did not show.

**Fixed.** `restore-svg-sizes.mjs` asks each page, in the browser, what every size-setting rule matches. A rule that matches only SVG text had its size put back to the value it had on main: 294 rules (Session 2: 2, Session 4: 144, Session 5: 144, promoted block: 4). Nothing else in those rules changed, so they keep the new face, weight and colours. D-136.

|---|---|---|---|
| SVG labels outside their drawing | 0 | 18 | 0 |
| Overlapping SVG label pairs | 85 | 112 | 82 |

**Left.** One element: on Session 5 at 390 px with everything revealed, one tile label in `#s3Inputs` measures 3 px taller than its box (3 such elements on main, 4 now). Registered under DW-214.

**Read by eye.** The full-length captures of Sessions 4 and 5 were tiled and read screen by screen (12 sheets), with samples of the others. No broken component found. One chart on Session 5 whose row labels cross its axis line was checked against main: identical before and after.
